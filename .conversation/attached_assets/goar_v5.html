Examples of how Goar’s tools can fit into a personal project, a small business or a team.

For developers and personal projects

Make a change without returning to your desk.

Open your project, edit files and compile an Android app on your phone. For work that needs more resources, connect to your own SSH host and run the build there.

Build compatibility depends on your project, toolchain and available device resources.

  1. 01
    Open and editUse Files, the code editor and Terminal.
  2. 02
    Build where it makes senseUse the local sandbox or an external machine you configure.
  3. 03
    Keep the output with your projectAccess the compiled APK from Files.

For shops, freelancers and marketing teams

Prepare customer outreach in the same workspace.

Manage an audience and email campaign through your SMTP server. Use your Twilio account for SMS, or your Telegram bot for channel posts and messages.

Sending requires configured services. Account fees, consent requirements and provider limits still apply.

  1. 01
    Choose the audienceUse your email audience and campaign tools.
  2. 02
    Prepare the messageWork on the copy in chat before using a sending tool.
  3. 03
    Use your own channelsEmail through SMTP, SMS through Twilio, or posts through Telegram.

For service businesses and operations teams

Set up repeatable payment tasks.

Goar includes Stripe and PayPal tools for charging and payouts. Connect your accounts and configure the payment work you want the agent to carry out.

Use payment automation only with appropriate authorisation. Provider fees, eligibility and transaction rules apply.

  1. 01
    Connect the payment serviceUse your own Stripe or PayPal account.
  2. 02
    Define the taskSpecify the intended charge or payout and its conditions.
  3. 03
    Check the resultVerify transactions in your payment provider’s records.

For agencies and company teams

Share the workspace behind the work.

Share workspaces, import profiles from another app installation and use skills as repeatable instructions. Connect the external tools your team already uses through MCP.

Review company security requirements before sharing profiles, workspace data or connecting accounts.

  1. 01
    Bring in a profileImport an existing setup rather than starting again.
  2. 02
    Share the workUse workspace sharing and playbooks for common tasks.
  3. 03
    Connect your servicesAdd MCP tools or use Telegram for team messages.

Keep the project with you

Continue the work. Keep a copy.

Your files, conversations and settings stay accessible on your phone. Data controls provide exports, imports and backups for moving or restoring your setup.

Keep backup copies outside the device as well. A backup stored only on a lost or damaged phone cannot protect you from losing that phone.

Scheduled work
Tasks, Watchtower and overnight runs let you configure recurring work. Local execution depends on Android background settings and device availability.
Memory and skills
Keep notes across chats and give the agent playbooks to follow.
Browser and desktop
Use the in-app browser or connect to a Linux desktop through VNC over SSH.
Images and video
Use the providers you configure for creative work, alongside your project files and conversations.
